## Suggested course flow

1. Calibrate the audience and decision.
2. Demonstrate a complete positive and degraded path.
3. Let the learner run a bounded failure-driven lab.
4. Assess the learner's evidence and explanation.
5. Calibrate facilitators before repeating the delivery.
6. Collect only the adoption evidence needed for the next revision.

This flow is a teaching workflow, not a claim that a course has measured
learning impact. The evidence boundary remains the same as in the linked
repositories: synthetic examples are useful for instruction but do not prove
live production quality or learner outcomes beyond the declared activity.
