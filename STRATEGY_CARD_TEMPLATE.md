# Strategy card template

## Strategy

Name the reusable teaching move in one sentence.

## Audience and context

State who the strategy serves, what they already know, and when it is useful.

## Teaching problem

Describe the learner failure that occurs when the strategy is missing.

## Facilitator move

Describe what the instructor, author, or course designer does.

## Learner evidence

Name the observable artifact or behavior that demonstrates progress. Separate
delivery evidence from outcome evidence.

## Feedback and adaptation

Explain how the evidence changes the next teaching action.

## Accessibility

State how the strategy works for different access needs, formats, pace, and
prior experience.

## Responsible-use boundary

State what the learner must not infer from the exercise and what data must stay
private or synthetic.

## Verification

Name the check, rubric, replay, or human review that confirms the strategy was
applied as intended.

## What this does and does not prove

State the bounded conclusion and the claims that remain unestablished.
